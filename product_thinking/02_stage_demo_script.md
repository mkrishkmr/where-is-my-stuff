# 3-Minute Stage Demo Script — Family Edition

## Setup & Props
- **Stage Prop:** 1 small pouch with 3 items (Passport, HDMI Cable, Keys).
- **Accounts:** Logged in as `alex@family.com` (showing Shared Household: "The Sharma Family").
- **Second Tab:** Incognito window ready to paste a shared link.

---

## 3-Minute Stage Pitch Script

### Act 1: The Family Problem (0:00 - 0:25)
- **Speaker:** "How many times a week do you get a text from your partner, kid, or roommate asking: *'Where did you put the passport?'* or *'Where are the spare keys?'* The problem isn't that things don't have a place—it's that telling your family is high-friction, and when someone borrows something, they never put it back."

### Act 2: Smooth 1-Tap Capture (0:25 - 1:00)
- **Action:** Open app on stage. Tap **Capture** tab.
- **Speaker:** "Adding a location must take under 5 seconds, or nobody does it."
- **Action:** Snap photo or tap **"Sample Candle"**. The app immediately sends the picture to Opus 5.5 for real-time analysis.
- **Screen:** A dedicated single-item review screen displays the detected item name, visual proof, and location pills.
- **Action:** Tap **"Confirm & Add to Catalogue"**. Item is added instantly. Zero typing.

### Act 3: Seamless Humanlike Retrieval (1:00 - 1:40)
- **Action:** Type into chat: *"Where did we leave that black TV cord?"*
- **Speaker:** "No rigid keyword searching. Family members ask naturally."
- **Screen:** Assistant replies warmly: *"Your Braided HDMI Cable is in the Office desk drawer, left compartment. Here is the photo from yesterday."*

### Act 4: "Put It Back" Return Timer (1:40 - 2:15)
- **Action:** Click **"Borrow & Remind"** on the HDMI cable. Select **"2 Hours"**.
- **Speaker:** "I'm borrowing the cable. Instead of it vanishing into thin air, the app sets a return timer. When it expires, it pings my phone: *'Time to return the HDMI cable back to the Office desk drawer.'*"

### Act 5: 1-Click Family Share & Email Wall (2:15 - 2:45)
- **Action:** Click **"Share with Family"** on the Passport item. Copy link.
- **Action:** Paste into incognito tab. Shows quick email login (`mom@family.com`). Once unlocked, immediately displays the Passport's exact drawer location and photo proof.
- **Speaker:** "Instant family transparency. Anyone in the household with an email can see the exact spot without texting you."

### Act 6: Wrap-up (2:45 - 3:00)
- **Speaker:** "Smooth capture, humanlike answers, return timers, and family sharing. Where Is My Stuff ends household hide-and-seek."
