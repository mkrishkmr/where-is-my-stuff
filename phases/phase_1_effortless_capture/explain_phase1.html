The Capture Paradox (Why Apps Die)

People put items away during low-attention moments (hands full, arriving home, tired). Traditional apps demand typing titles, tags, categories, and locations. Each required field adds friction. Cumulatively, this creates form fatigue: users abandon the app in week one.

The Opus 5.5 Solution

1 Tap Photo + 1 Tap Location Pill. The user never types an item name. Divyam Claude Opus 5.5 inspects the photo asynchronously, extracting 3 to 8 retrievable physical items with visual attributes in under 2 seconds.
