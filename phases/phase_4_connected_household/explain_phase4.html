Phase 4 Target: Shared Domestic Map
The Single-Memory Bottleneck

In most homes, one person knows where everything lives. Everyone else depends on them, causing recurring interruptions: "Where is the spare house key?" texted during meetings, followed by verbal ambiguity: "In the drawer" (which drawer?).

The 1-Click Family Share Solution

Turning one person's visual memory into a shared household resource. A family member clicks "Share with Family", sends a direct link via WhatsApp or iMessage, and the recipient sees the exact room, drawer, and photo proof in under 1 second.
