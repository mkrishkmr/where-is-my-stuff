The Displaced Item Trap

Belongings rarely vanish into thin air. They get displaced. A family member borrows a charger, takes it to the couch or car, and leaves it there. It becomes findable only by someone who remembers the full borrowing history—which is the exact person who forgot.

1-Click Borrow & Remind

Tapping "1h" or "24h" assigns the return to a specific person and due time. The entire household can see who has it, eliminating the recurring friction of: "Where did you leave my cable this time?"
